Get yggstore

Paste the line from your invite into a terminal, on Linux or macOS:

curl -fsSL https://get.withcare.co.za/install.sh | sh -s -- 'yggjoin1:…'

It installs yggstore, joins the group and opens your dashboard. No admin password is needed.

The programs: Linux, Linux on ARM, macOS (Apple), macOS (Intel), and their checksums. More about yggstore: github.com/peterretief/yggstore.
