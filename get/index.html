Get yggstore

Host websites and email for yourself and your clients on small, low-power devices, from anywhere. Boxes link up over any network, with no public IP, and spread encrypted pieces across members, so power cuts, bad networks or a lost machine don't take anything down. It's a co-op: give storage or pay those who do.

Just want to try it? Make a free account: a small website and 500 MB for your files, in your browser. Nothing to install.

Paste the line from your invite into a terminal, on Linux or macOS:

curl -fsSL https://get.withcare.co.za/install.sh | sh -s -- 'yggjoin1:…'

It installs yggstore, joins the group and opens your dashboard. No admin password is needed.

No invite yet? Apply to join.

The programs: Linux, Linux on ARM, macOS (Apple), macOS (Intel), and their checksums. More about yggstore: github.com/peterretief/yggstore, and a map of its code.
