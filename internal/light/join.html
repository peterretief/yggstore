Open your files

This sets up this phone or computer to open your files on the box, from anywhere, in the browser.

You'll make a passkey: your device asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and that's how you sign in from now on. There's no password to remember. The invite link works once.

Your files are stored encrypted on the group's machines. Over the web, what you open or upload passes through Cloudflare and one of the group's web nodes on its way to your box.
